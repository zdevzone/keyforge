# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith, is
probably tired, and may not share your first language.

## What is expected

- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Keyforge aims to stay small
  and readable; "no" to a feature is usually about scope, not about you.
- When you disagree, say what you would do instead.

## What is not

Harassment, sustained personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery,
publishing anyone's private information, or deliberately derailing discussion.

## Reporting

Email <keyforge.security@zdevzone.com>. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential. There is no committee; expect a human reply within a week.

Consequences scale with the behavior — a request to stop, a temporary block, or
a permanent one. The maintainer decides, and will say why.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.
