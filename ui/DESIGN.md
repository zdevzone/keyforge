# Design System: Keyforge Console — Polaris

## Overview

The Backups surface adapts to host capabilities without changing Polaris styling. Serverless hosting explains its daily check time and external archive requirement, disables local-only/upload-only actions, and keeps authenticated download and recovery verification in the shared history table. Persistent-host interactions remain unchanged. See `docs/vercel.md` for deployment and recovery details.

**Creative North Star: "The Licensing Operations Workspace"**

The user-selected Polaris theme from @shadcnblocks/theme/polaris, authored by tweakcn, is the visual authority. Cool neutral surfaces, teal actions, amber secondary accents and square geometry support a precise, calm workspace. Google Sans Flex is hosted locally. The application uses paid shadcnblocks source compositions adapted to real Keyforge records and workflows.

Operators repeatedly find licenses, inspect state, manage seats, answer support and review usage or sales. The interface keeps those tasks legible in both light and dark appearances, with compact navigation, bounded listings and focused dialogs. The chosen Polaris direction supersedes the earlier Figma/Inter/blue and UI Arc visual guidance.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Cool light surfaces and deep blue-neutral dark surfaces with teal primary actions.
- Amber secondary accents, explicit semantic statuses and square base geometry.
- Locally hosted Google Sans Flex with tabular numbers and monospace identifiers.
- Compact grouped navigation, scrollable tables and internally scrolling dialogs.
- App-owned shadcnblocks adaptations built on upgradeable generated shadcn primitives.

## Colors

Polaris pairs muted teal and warm amber with cool neutral surfaces. The frontmatter preserves the exact source OKLCH strings from `src/index.css`; unprefixed tokens describe light appearance and `dark-*` tokens describe the corresponding dark overrides. Matching values may have distinct semantic roles.

### Primary

- **Polaris Teal** (`primary`): primary actions, active navigation, selected tabs and charts. Use the matching `primary-foreground` for text on a solid primary surface.
- **Clear Teal** (`accent`) and **Focus Teal** (`ring`): accent treatments and keyboard focus. Selection in menus uses the quieter `muted` surface; active navigation mixes primary into the sidebar.

### Secondary

- **Warm Amber** (`secondary`): secondary emphasis and its matching foreground. Amber is part of the selected theme; warning status retains its separate semantic token.

### Neutral

- **Cool Canvas** (`background`), **Panel** (`card`) and **Floating Panel** (`popover`): layered surfaces with matching foregrounds.
- **Operational Ink** (`foreground`) and **Quiet Ink** (`muted-foreground`): main text and supporting content.
- **Muted Surface** (`muted`), **Divider** (`border`) and **Field Edge** (`input`): group related content, separate rows and identify controls.
- Sidebar roles preserve their own surface, foreground, active, accent, border and focus mappings.

Success, warning and destructive tokens always accompany readable text. Chart colors distinguish series; meter units remain separate. Existing slate utilities are compatibility aliases to these semantic roles, not an independent palette.

**The Semantic Pair Rule.** Use each semantic surface with its matching foreground and preserve the separate light and dark mappings.

ThemeControl applies the root dark class and browser color scheme. Appearance persists locally under `keyforge.theme`, synchronizes across tabs and initially follows the system when no saved choice exists. Header and Settings use the same state.

## Typography

**Display and Body Font:** Google Sans Flex Variable, locally imported through `@fontsource-variable/google-sans-flex`, with the frontmatter's system sans fallbacks. Identifiers and public keys use the existing monospace utility; quantities use tabular numerals.

The normative hierarchy is in the frontmatter. Page titles use title on desktop and title-mobile below the desktop-navigation breakpoint. Metrics similarly use metric and metric-mobile. Dialog titles, larger sections, ordinary sections and compact sections remain distinct. Body and field labels are readable operational text; navigation uses its compact role. Small, micro and counter roles support table headings, timestamps, group labels, keyboard hints and notification counts. The tiny counter is reserved for the bell count rather than body copy. Mobile text entry uses mobile-input.

The observed ramp intentionally includes the detector-reported sizes 10, 11, 15, 24, 26, 28 and 30, alongside 12, 13, 14, 16, 18 and 20. Older overridden CSS declarations do not define the rendered hierarchy; later Polaris rules govern page titles, summary labels and metrics.

**The Operational Type Rule.** Use the documented compact annotation roles for metadata and the page-title and metric roles for hierarchy; do not flatten them into one size.

The public welcome surface uses the scoped public-brand, public-display and public-section roles from the frontmatter. Its display switches to public-display-tablet at a maximum width of (1100px), then public-display-mobile at a maximum width of (767px); the brand uses the existing (20px) size on mobile. Public section headings retain their fluid role. These additions describe the welcome page's larger reading hierarchy; the console keeps its operational ramp.

**The Public Type Scope Rule.** Apply the public display and section roles within the welcome surface; retain operational title, metric and annotation roles in the console.

The public documentation surface extends Polaris with a scoped reading hierarchy: body text at (16px) with (1.75) line height and a maximum measure of (72ch), article titles at (32px) with (1.2) line height, second-level headings at (24px), third-level headings at (20px), and monospace code blocks at (13px). Below (640px), article titles use (28px) and second-level headings use (22px). The (32px) title is an intentional documentation reading role, rather than a change to the console ramp. Semantic color pairs, locally hosted Google Sans Flex, square controls and existing overlay behavior carry through; the responsive navigation, content boundary and finish evidence belong to [the documentation brief](.impeccable/surfaces/docs.md).

## Layout

ApplicationShell1 sets a sidebar width of (15rem) and collapsed rail of (3.5rem). The sticky desktop header has a minimum height of (64px) and horizontal padding of (28px). Content is centered at a maximum (1600px), with desktop padding (32px 28px 48px). The header orders product, theme, bell and avatar; command search remains available alongside route context.

Summary cards form four columns on desktop and two on mobile. Lists use wrapping task controls, filters, table headings and right-aligned actions. The shared table has a header height of (44px), normal row height of (60px), and cell padding of (16px). Dialogs stay centered, scroll internally and fit within (90dvh), with a large-screen maximum width of (42rem). Labels stay above their controls at every width. Related fields use the shared responsive form group: columns fit when each has at least (15rem), with a (20px) gap, and stack when the container is narrower. Route context hides below (1100px) to leave room for header controls.

The public welcome layout uses a centered maximum width of (1320px), a split copy/console-preview hero, and broader section spacing. At its tablet breakpoint the four-step walkthrough becomes two columns; on mobile the hero, walkthrough, feature, integration and FAQ layouts stack, navigation uses a Sheet and actions stretch across the available width. ## Elevation & Depth

Borders and tonal layering establish depth. Cards and buttons have no resting shadow. Dialogs use a restrained overlay and elevated shadow; floating shadcn popovers retain Polaris shadow tokens. The sidecar records exact shadows, overlay and focus snippets. Reduced-motion preferences set both animation and transition duration to (0ms), including theme changes.

**The Quiet Surface Rule.** Keep operational cards and buttons flat at rest; reserve elevation for dialogs and floating controls.

The public console-preview figure carries a soft shadow to separate the product raster from the page. Its one-time entrance settles into place and is disabled for reduced motion. Both treatments are scoped to that figure and recorded in the sidecar.

## Shapes

The Polaris base radius is square. App-owned styling applies it to cards, buttons, inputs, textareas, selectors, tabs and dialogs. Avatars, status pills, notification counts and spinners retain their purposeful round silhouettes. Borders and row dividers define structure without decorative geometry. Generated radius utilities are implementation details; the console's base geometry is the authority.

## Components

- **Authority and ownership:** `application-shell1`, `data-table12` and `settings-profile1` are app-owned shadcnblocks adaptations under `src/components`. Generated primitives remain under `src/components/ui` and are replaced on library upgrades. Existing inactive Arc/ReUI source remains on disk after automatic approval rejected recursive deletion; no runtime imports or styling authority remain.
- **Buttons:** primary, outlined and destructive variants have square geometry, concise labels, pending feedback, visible focus and disabled states. Shared controls have a minimum height of (36px); pagination controls have a minimum (38px) width and height. Outline and ghost hover use muted surfaces.
- **Fields and choices:** labelled fields use panel surfaces and readable error feedback. Text, date and choice controls have a minimum (40px) height. Focus has one continuous (2px) stroke at a (-1px) outline offset, without a second shadow ring. Command search gives the input group that stroke; its inner input has no separate outline. Buttons retain a separated keyboard outline. Choice uses shadcn Select for up to seven options and Command/Popover for larger catalogs or an explicit searchable override. Values come from supplied options, including intentional empty-ID choices such as manual sharing. Required searchable selection focuses its trigger and reports an error when no valid supplied option is selected.
- **Dates:** DateField uses shadcn Calendar in a Popover, with Today and Clear actions. It preserves calendar-date strings until the API interprets UTC expiry; local date choice must not introduce a timezone shift.
- **Cards and tables:** flat bordered panels, restrained headings, visible empty states and tabular values. The data-table12 adaptation uses TanStack Table v8 sorting, column visibility, proper headings and pagination after caller filtering. Actions and their headings align right; icon actions retain accessible names and tooltips.
- **Navigation and Settings:** grouped operational links, active-route state and mobile dismissal remain permission-aware. Reports contains Reports, admin-only Financial Report and feature-gated Audit. Settings consolidates Account, Preferences, Notifications, enabled admin-only Backups and admin-only System. Legacy profile, system, notifications and backup routes redirect to the matching tabs. Avatar-menu actions retain account identity, Settings and Sign out.
- **Profile:** settings-profile1 surrounds the real account workflow. ProfileImage uses shadcn Avatar, buttons and a native single-file input, with PNG/JPEG/WebP validation, a (5 MB) source limit, maximum (256px) dimension, processing feedback, preview, removal, initials fallback and explicit Save profile. Security, own sessions and the optional HTTPS image URL remain.
- **Notifications:** the shadcn Popover shows five recent personal notifications and View all. The table preserves read/unread state, current-page versus all-matching selection, confirmations and bulk API operations. Browser appearance and server-saved product notification preferences remain distinct; disabling capture does not erase history.
- **Support:** ConversationThread presents email-style history with shadcn Avatar and Badge. Internal notes stay separate from customer messages and explicitly private. Assignment, status, unread state, bounded history and the validated composer preserve the existing API workflow. The unavailable application-shell10 block is not claimed as installed.
- **Command search:** GlobalCommand provides Ctrl/Cmd K navigation and API-backed license results within the active product, with loading/error feedback. It does not interrupt an edit dialog. Direct list filters remain local inputs.
- **Dialogs and reports:** use the shared Radix-backed Modal for accessible titles, focus trapping, dismissal and focus return. Creation, edits, payments and confirmations use focused dialogs. Reports show real product/date/customer/status context, separate meter units and ETB amounts, and export all filtered rows through CSV or browser Print / Save PDF. Status reports are current-state; usage dates include whole overlapping buckets, and lifetime totals require no dates.

The complete screen inventory, source provenance, preserved behavior and known registry gaps are in [the redesign record](../docs/ui-redesign.md). Surface-specific workflow contracts remain in [the console brief](.impeccable/surfaces/console-patterns.md).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- Do use Polaris semantic tokens and the app-owned shell, table, settings and field adapters.
- Do keep focus, loading, error, empty, disabled and permission states visible.
- Do preserve real product scope, units, date meaning and complete exports.
- Do keep tables, Settings tabs, forms and dialog actions reachable on mobile.
- Do retain catalog-only selection, date-only calendar values and accessible row-action names.

### Don't:

- Don't reintroduce the retired Figma/Inter/blue or scoped UI Arc visual authority.
- Don't hand-edit generated shadcn primitives; style and adapt them in app-owned files.
- Don't invent records, totals, customer models or delivery actions from block demonstrations.
- Don't expose private signing material or make internal support notes appear customer-visible.
- Don't replace direct table filtering with global command search or append editing forms below listings.

### Compact listing controls

App-owned `.table-toolbar` and `.data-grid-tools` place filters, selects, dates, counts and table actions in a wrapping row immediately above results. Products supplies its search and status to the shared data-grid toolbar so Columns stays in that row. Short selects and buttons fit their labels; text fields are bounded to 28rem and numeric fields to 12rem, rather than stretching across a whole panel. Long message/token editors retain a readable 72ch bound. At narrow widths, controls wrap in DOM order without page overflow. Generated primitives remain unchanged.

Backup storage uses an admin-only focused dialog on the existing Backups tab. Credential fields are empty on edit, indicate retained values and do not expose saved secrets. Save, connection testing, default selection and archive verification remain distinct operations with feedback.
